### CHEM-000524: Closed-loop, energy-competitive lithium-mediated ammonia synthesis
- **Subfield:** Nitrogen fixation and ammonia chemistry
- **Open:** Close electrolyte, proton-carrier and lithium balances during continuous ammonia production from nitrogen and hydrogen, at total energy cost competitive with renewable-hydrogen Haber–Bosch.
- **Status:** A longstanding efficiency and scale problem. Li et al. demonstrated 300 hours in a 25 cm² flow electrolyser in 2024; Faradaic efficiency alone does not establish total energy competitiveness.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** Enables distributed fertilizer production with recyclable process materials.
- **Key works:** S. Li et al. (2024), *Long-term continuous ammonia electrosynthesis*, [DOI:10.1038/s41586-024-07276-5](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07276-5).
- **Related:** Chemical engineering: minimum-energy reactive separation. Energy systems: intermittent industrial-load scheduling.
